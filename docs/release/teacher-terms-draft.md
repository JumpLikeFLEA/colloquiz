# Teacher terms — DRAFT for the owner

Not published and not rendered anywhere. Not legal advice. A teacher accepts
these before an admin gives them a seat (docs/decisions/0170). Version 0.1,
2026-10-18.

## 1. What a seat is
A seat lets you build courses in Studio, link your students, write to them,
group them, assign homework and see their answers, until the seat's end date.
When it ends, Studio becomes read-only; published courses and your students'
access stay.

## 2. Who is responsible for what
Colloquiz is the controller of the data in the service. Inside the service we
are responsible for what you can see and do. What you do outside it (your own
lessons, payment you take through Patreon or a similar service, contact
details a student gives you directly) is your own responsibility.

## 3. What you may do with student data
You may use what you see in Colloquiz to teach that student, in the app or in
your own lessons outside it.

You may not:
- export it, or copy it into your own files or systems;
- share it with anyone else;
- use it for marketing, or for anything other than teaching that student;
- keep using it after the student's link to you ends.

## 4. Students
You link only students who chose to link to you. You do not create accounts
for students or upload lists of students. Do not pressure a student to link,
stay linked or share more than the link shows.

## 5. Your content
The content you write is yours. Students who have bought access keep it, as
the Terms of Service say.

## 6. Reports and removal
Anyone may report a teacher at privacy@colloquiz.app. We may freeze your seat
at once while we look into a report. If you misuse student data we treat it as
a personal data breach and may notify the supervisory authority and the
students affected. We may end a seat for breaking these terms.

## 7. Your own data
We hold your name, contact, seat notes and expiry. The notes are included in
your data export.

## Open for the owner
- Governing law, liability wording and fees are not drafted here.
- Whether acceptance is a checkbox in `/studio/authors` or a signed email.
